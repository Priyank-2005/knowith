Methodology and interpretation

Exactly six rounds

Each game selects one scenario from each of six curated regime pools and then shuffles the order. The game code makes that selection reproducible.

Mixed evidence windows

Rounds use two-, three-, four- or five-year lookbacks. Every game contains at least one two-year and one five-year lens, plus at least one additional horizon. The displayed cumulative return compounds the annual basket returns; CAGR annualises that cumulative result.

Four analytical baskets

  • India: India.
  • US: USA.
  • DM ex USA: UK, France, Germany, Japan, Singapore and Hong Kong.
  • EM ex India: China, Taiwan and Korea.

Media narratives

Published headlines are included to recreate the emotional decision environment. Before the bet, calendar-year references may be lightly redacted and publication details are hidden. After the bet, the exact headline, outlet, source link and publication or archive context are revealed. Headlines are context—not a backtested signal.

Full data reveal

After the sixth round, an animated sequence flashes every year and every country return, followed by a persistent heatmap of all 286 observations. The purpose is to expose how easy it is to remember a compelling episode while overlooking the full distribution of outcomes.
